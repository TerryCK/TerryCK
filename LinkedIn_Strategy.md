## 5. Recruiter roleplay — brutally honest audit

*Roleplaying a senior technical recruiter at a high-growth, remote-first AI/consumer startup, reading your profile cold.*

**Would I reach out? — Yes, but conditionally.** The mobile + AI-agent combination is genuinely rare and exactly what my hiring managers ask for in 2026, and 16K+ ratings on solo-shipped apps is real, verifiable proof of end-to-end ownership. You'd clear my first screen. But a few things would make me hesitate before spending a hiring manager's time, and at a FAANG-tier or top AI lab they could be dealbreakers.

**The 5 biggest red flags**

1. **"Tap" is unverifiable as written.** You cite "10 billion downloads," but I can't confirm your *individual* contribution or even pin down the company (Tap is a common name). A skeptical recruiter reads big portfolio numbers as borrowed credibility. **Fix:** add a company link/logo on LinkedIn, confirm your exact title and start year (the resume still has `[confirm start year]` placeholders — never publish those), and frame metrics as *what you* shipped, not what the portfolio totals.

2. **Seniority is asserted, not evidenced.** You call yourself "Senior" but the profile shows individual feature work, not scope signals — mentoring, leading a workstream, owning architecture decisions, cross-team influence. **Fix:** add 1–2 bullets showing leadership/ownership scope, not just output.

3. **The AI work reads as self-directed, not production-proven.** "Agentic workflows I built for myself" is impressive but ambiguous — did this ship to users or just speed up your own coding? AI-native companies want AI *in the product*. **Fix:** sharpen the Luna AI-character bullet (that's your strongest "AI shipped to users" proof) and quantify the workflow impact.

4. **No metrics on the AI/efficiency claims.** "Ships apps faster" with no number is a soft claim. Recruiters and HMs discount unquantified impact. **Fix:** add even rough figures — "cut release time ~30%," "reduced manual QA steps," "N apps maintained solo."

5. **Geography/timezone friction is unaddressed.** Taiwan/APAC + remote is fine for some, a blocker for others. If your profile doesn't state work-authorization/overlap expectations, recruiters for US/EU teams may skip rather than ask. **Fix:** one line — "open to remote; comfortable with X hours overlap" — removes the guesswork.

**Net:** Strong, differentiated, top-quartile for a mobile+AI hybrid — *once* the Tap details are verified and the senior/impact claims are backed with numbers. Right now it reads as "very promising, needs proof."

## 7. Three cold DM templates for recruiters

Short, specific, no corporate fluff. Reference something concrete, lead with your rarest value, end with one frictionless ask. Keep each under ~75 words. `[brackets]` = personalize before sending.

**Template A — warm/specific (you saw a role or their post)**
> Hi [Name] — saw [Company] is hiring for [role / saw your post on mobile+AI]. Quick reason I'm reaching out: I'm a senior mobile engineer who also builds the AI agent workflows that ship apps faster — currently doing both at Tap (10B+ download portfolio), with 16K+ App Store ratings on apps I built solo. Seems like a strong fit for what you're building. Open to a 15-min chat this week?

**Template B — value-first (no specific role yet)**
> Hi [Name] — I build consumer apps end-to-end (iOS/Android/backend) *and* the multi-model AI agent automation behind them. Most teams hire two people for that; I'm one. 16K+ ratings on my own apps, now shipping at 10B-download scale. If [Company] is thinking about AI in your mobile products, I'd love to be on your radar — worth a quick call?

**Template C — ultra-short (busy recruiter / InMail reply)**
> Hi [Name] — senior iOS/mobile engineer + AI-agent builder (rare combo). 16K+ App Store ratings solo; now at Tap (10B+ downloads). Targeting remote AI/consumer roles. Is [role/team] still open? Happy to send specifics.

**Send tips:** personalize the first line every time (recruiters spot a blast instantly), send Tue–Thu mornings their timezone, and if no reply in ~5 days, one short follow-up max.
